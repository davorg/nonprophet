   The carousel wording says “every prophet does this.” That overstates the case. The packet supports that speaking God’s words is the ordinary prophetic role, not literally that every prophet in every episode does so in the same way.

   Material effect: Moderate. The verdict category can stand, but the wording should distinguish “non-unique” from “not independent at all.”
