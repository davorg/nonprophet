# Editorial audit: claims 041–045

Claims 041–043 split Deuteronomy's prophet passage into separate propositions.
They should not be allowed to multiply one disputed identification into three
independent proofs.

| Claim | Strongest connection | Main audit question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 041 | Jesus says Moses wrote about him | Does John identify Deuteronomy 18 specifically? |
| 042 | Both prophets and Jesus speak words supplied by God | Is this unique to one final prophet? |
| 043 | Acts explicitly applies the warning to its proclamation of Jesus | Why does Acts strengthen Deuteronomy's wording to “cut off”? |
| 044 | Paul explicitly applies the curse of a displayed corpse to the crucified Jesus | Is Paul's theological rereading an advance prediction? |
| 045 | Joshua meets the LORD's commander; Hebrews calls Jesus salvation's *archēgos* | Are the figures actually identified, or does the claim depend on translating two different titles as “captain”? |
