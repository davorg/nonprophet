# Editorial audit: claims 046–050

This batch moves from narrative typology into promises about Israel's priesthood
and monarchy. The audit keeps an immediate historical referent separate from a
later Christian application.

| Claim | Strongest connection | Main audit question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 046 | Boaz and Christ are both described as redeemers | Does Ruth present Boaz as a prediction, and is Ephesians using kinship redemption? |
| 047 | Jesus is a faithful and permanent high priest | Does 1 Samuel point beyond the replacement of Eli's priestly house? |
| 048 | Hannah anticipates a divinely empowered anointed king | Does the royal ideal identify Jesus rather than Israel's coming monarchy generally? |
| 049 | Matthew explicitly presents Jesus as David's descendant | Is 2 Samuel's immediate promise to Solomon also a wider dynastic promise? |
| 050 | Both texts speak of an enduring kingdom | Does “forever” describe one immortal king or the Davidic throne and dynasty? |
