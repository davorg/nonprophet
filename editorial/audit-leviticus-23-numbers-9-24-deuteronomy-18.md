# Editorial audit: claims 036–040

| Claim | Original passage | Strongest Christian connection | Main audit question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 036 | Leviticus orders festival offerings, including drink offerings | Jesus offers living water during the same festival | Is the later water-pouring ceremony the same thing as Leviticus's drink offering? |
| 037 | Numbers forbids breaking the Passover animal's bones | John says Jesus's unbroken bones fulfil Scripture | Does explicit NT fulfilment make the meal rule a prediction? |
| 038 | Looking at the raised bronze snake preserves life | John directly compares it with the raised Son of Man | Is this explicit typology or original-context prediction? |
| 039 | Balaam sees a future victorious ruler | Christians identify that ruler with Jesus | Do the supplied NT verses make that identification, and is “not now” a useful time marker? |
| 040 | Moses promises a prophet or succession of prophets | John calls Jesus “the Prophet”; Acts quotes the promise about him | Does the original passage mean one final prophet or Israel's prophetic institution? |

Claims 037 and 038 have unusually strong New Testament links. Their main issue is
classification: explicit Christian fulfilment does not necessarily mean that the
earlier passage functioned as a direct prediction.
