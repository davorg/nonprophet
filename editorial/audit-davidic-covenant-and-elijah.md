# Editorial audit: claims 051–055

Four claims continue the Davidic-covenant material from claims 049–050. Claim
054 is the Chronicler's parallel to claim 049 and must not be counted as an
independent prediction. Claim 053 is a narrative comparison rather than a royal
promise.

| Claim | Strongest connection | Main audit question |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 051 | Hebrews explicitly quotes the father-son formula about Jesus | Does royal sonship in Samuel mean divine identity, and what does the omitted discipline clause show? |
| 052 | Jesus is presented as David's descendant and enduring king | Does the promise concern one person or David's dynasty? |
| 053 | Elijah and Jesus are both carried into heaven before witnesses | Does Luke intend an Elijah pattern, and would that make the older story a prediction? |
| 054 | Matthew presents Jesus as David's son | Is this anything more than claim 049 repeated from Chronicles? |
| 055 | Luke closely echoes the Davidic throne, sonship and forever-kingdom complex | How do Solomon's immediate role and Chronicles's edited form affect the claim? |
