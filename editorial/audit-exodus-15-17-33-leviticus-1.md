# Editorial audit: claims 026–030

These are five individual claims, not five proofs of a single Exodus prediction.

| Claim | Original passage | Strongest Christian connection | Main audit question |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 026 | Exodus 15:2 celebrates YHWH's rescue at the sea | Jesus's name shares the salvation root; Stephen sees him exalted | Does a common noun or a right-hand motif forecast his name or exaltation? |
| 027 | Exodus 15:11 praises YHWH's unique holiness | Luke calls Jesus holy; Acts calls him God's holy servant | Is the hymn's description of God a prediction of a future servant? |
| 028 | Exodus 17:6 narrates water from a rock | Paul explicitly says the spiritual rock was Christ | Does Paul's canonical identification make the earlier narrative a prospective prediction? |
| 029 | Exodus 33:19 is God's reply to Moses's request for glory | The NT associates Christ with God's grace and mercy | Is Luke 1:72's grammatical subject Jesus, and how does Romans 9:15 quote Exodus? |
| 030 | Leviticus 1:2–9 regulates a burnt offering | Ephesians 5:2 uses the pleasing-aroma sacrifice formula for Christ | Does this recurring ritual formula identify a unique future sacrifice? |

Claim 030's inherited list uses Leviticus 1:2–9. A separate 365-item apologist list assigns its claim 30 to Leviticus 14:11; do not silently transfer that different citation to this claim.
